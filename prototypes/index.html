snowprofile prototypes

Static views for settling layout and flow before the app builds them. The bar at the top of each page switches its language, role, and state. See prototypes/README.md.

  • Sign-in: demo mode, Google only, refused domain, wrong password
  • No access: signed in without a membership
  • My profile, in the app frame: personal details, education, participations, own projects, the update request
  • People: last confirmations, update requests, leavers (admin)
  • Projects: the project list, with filters and missing translations
  • Project editing: every field, contact persons, technologies, criteria answers (admin)
  • Project view: what a participant reads, and what others don't see
  • Search: people by technology and period (admin)
  • CV: selection, missing translations, the table to copy into Word or Google Docs (admin)
  • Members: member list, role change, invitation links (admin)
  • Technologies: catalogue by category, add, edit, merge duplicates
  • Roles: project roles in both languages, missing translations, merge duplicates (admin)
  • Technical characteristics: the checklist, its order, and its editing (admin)
